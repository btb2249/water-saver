Privacy Policy — Water Saver

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Water Saver is a homeowner's water-saving guide by Bethel Ventures. It helps you find leaks, fix the little stuff, and save water everywhere — plain and simple. Everything stays on your phone: no accounts, no ads, no data collected.

What the app does

Water Saver includes leak-detection checks (a water-meter test and a toilet dye test), a leak-spot checklist, plain step-by-step fix-it guides, water-saving tips for every room and the yard, runoff-reduction ideas (rain gardens, bioswales, and permeable pavers), and a personal monthly water log where you can record your water bill and the fixes you have made. All of it works entirely on your device.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. Water Saver does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It does not create an account for you, and it does not ask you to sign in.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in the app — your monthly water-log entries and the fixes you record — is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, that information is deleted with it.

Information we share

We share nothing, because we collect nothing. Water Saver sends no data to Bethel Ventures, to any analytics service, to any advertising network, or to any other third party.

Analytics and advertising

Water Saver contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party software that collects information about you.

Children

Water Saver is a general household guide and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and change the “Last updated” date above. The app itself will not change how it handles your information without that notice.
