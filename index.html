Water Saver

Find leaks, fix the little stuff, and save water everywhere — plain and simple.

Water Saver is a homeowner's water-saving guide by Bethel Ventures. Find leaks with a meter test and a toilet dye test, check ten common leak spots, follow plain fix-it guides, save water in every room and the yard, cut runoff with rain gardens and permeable pavers, and keep a monthly water log. Everything stays on your device: no accounts, no ads, no data collected.
